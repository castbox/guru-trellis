# Solution-mechanism applicability

The original caller reads this short contract before loading this qualifier's
`SKILL.md` or full contract. The caller judges whether dedicated mechanism
admission is needed; it does not decide mechanism suitability here.

Invoke for a proposed or materially changed mechanism that actually carries
business authority to decide, prove, reject or permit execution, or for an
observed authority placement conflict. This includes identity, state ownership,
concurrency, cancellation/recovery, inspection/monitoring, publication and
evidence authority. Judge the real responsibility, beyond OS or process words.
Ordinary calculation, text/navigation changes and state I/O without such
authority do not trigger merely because they can affect correctness.

For the same mechanism, adopt a still-applicable conclusion available in the
conversation or an original owner gate that already needs it. A stage, caller,
HEAD or unrelated file change alone does not repeat admission. Material changes
to scope, authority, mechanism, supported dependency/caller/consumer, conditions or
evidence refresh the affected judgment. Obtain a lost necessary conclusion
from its original owner or judge it again from live sources; never infer pass.

When invocation is needed, explicitly load the original Skill and complete
`contract.md`, submit the actual applicable nonempty candidate set, and consume
its original exit and unique consumer. Uncertain applicability with a real
mechanism enters this owner; unavailable facts keep their original gap route.
When not triggered, do not load the full qualifier, assemble input, invoke an
empty check or create a classified/skipped result. The caller still performs
its own stage judgment. New invocations bind current facts; adoption never
replays an earlier invocation envelope. No applicability artifact is written.
