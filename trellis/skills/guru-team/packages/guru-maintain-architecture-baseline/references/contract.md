# Architecture Baseline: Planning 3.0 and Nonplanning 6.0

## Authority model

The business repository Architecture Baseline is the only shared architecture
SSOT. It owns current/target state, decisions, GAPs, owners, compatibility
exits, ADRs, history, evidence, and the design constitution or its unique
locator. Guru Team owns the mandatory lifecycle and typed routes, not the
project's architecture answer.

The constitution projection contains one explicit `current` or
`pending_reviewed_promotion` authority status, one authority locator, one
version or content identity, and exactly these five stable identity/name pairs:

- `mature-practice-applicability` / `成熟实践与适用性`
- `concept-semantic-completeness` / `概念与语义完整性`
- `cohesion-change-isolation` / `职责内聚与变化隔离`
- `minimum-necessary-complexity` / `最小必要复杂度`
- `debt-one-way-convergence` / `技术债务单向收敛`

Principle prose, scores, per-principle verdicts, and mechanical checklists are
not public Skill data.

### Adopted-mechanism review

Review mechanisms newly introduced, modified, reused or used more widely by the
candidate, together with their direct effects and consumers. A mechanism is a
choice carrying behavior or constraints, including data authority, responsibility,
coordination, failure handling or lifecycle. Reuse can bring those constraints
into a new path even when the original utility has no diff. Ordinary helpers
and causally unrelated dependencies do not each require a review unit. No actual
Architecture impact retains the original lightweight route.

First reconstruct supported observable completion and its necessary business,
data, permission and external constraints. Derive a direct feasible completion
path before choosing coordination or recovery machinery. Compare the complete
path to the caller's promised result; an earlier acceptance response alone does
not establish lower completion latency.

Additional shared writes, serialization, waiting stages and independently
maintained state are inadmissible by default unless the current assessment
establishes why a simpler path cannot satisfy an accepted requirement. This
applies to new, modified, extended and reused choices. Purpose, generic safety
claims, existing technical rules and tests asserting a mechanism do not establish
admission. Do not classify necessity from mechanism names.

The author first attempts removal, narrower consistency scope, existing atomic
capabilities or a more direct responsibility boundary. The independent reviewer
actively attempts simpler alternatives and judges their feasibility instead of
only checking the author's argument for consistency. When the original choice
is already minimal or no simpler alternative is feasible, explain the actual
constraints; do not invent a feasible replacement to satisfy the review.

After locating the design basis, judge correctness and completion cost together.
Establish the actual behavior served; who decides, supplies facts and maintains
state; and which objects and periods the mechanism coordinates or rejects.
Examine added I/O, shared write contention, sequential dependencies, queue
waiting and recovery prerequisites across affected users, resources and process
instances. Retain necessary constraints at their minimum justified scope and
cost. Reject avoidable coordination cost on supported normal requests even when
functional assertions pass. Fewer lines or a faster acceptance response cannot
justify moving that cost to another stage or owner.

Compare removal, narrowing, replacement or retention using risk-matched evidence.
These are focused reasoning questions, not a required questionnaire, a new
approval stage, a global optimization exercise or a performance benchmark for
every helper. Finally check effects on legitimate normal requests.

Use applicable complete RDT and Architecture units under the shared
current-knowledge contract. Sufficient documentation can establish capability,
responsibility and applicability without repeated source archaeology. Read
implementation selectively when actual behavior, performance, the complete set
of affected consumers or conflicting documentation leaves a material gap. Finding
an explanation of how the mechanism works does not establish its necessity.
Reuse an applicable prior rationale and engineering facts concisely when they
cover the same constraints; stage or version changes alone do not require all
commands or all reasoning to be repeated.

Preserve real business and external contracts when comparing alternatives. When
an existing technical rule is itself under review, it cannot prove its own
necessity or exclude an alternative merely for omitting that rule. Evaluate the
candidate through the original reviewed-change contract, implement through its
lawful owner, and promote at the original stage. Do not change CURRENT first to
make the candidate conform or demand early promotion before implementation.
Existing, platform-native or installed capabilities and new code all require
current applicability. New abstractions, wrappers, conversions, configuration
and future provisions require a present responsibility and direct consumer.
Fewer keys or lines alone do not show lower total cost, especially when a new
ledger or manual coordination carries the displaced burden. A smaller diff must
not omit necessary responsibility boundaries, consumer adaptation or old-path exit.

For new or strengthened protection, consume the original
`guru-qualify-root-cause` contract's Protection judgment. Its proposer, including
an author, reviewer or template, bears the evidence burden. A missing technical
measure alone does not create a finding or validation obligation. Necessary
confidentiality or permission protection does not require a prior incident.
Protection eligibility alone does not approve the Architecture choice; apply
the simpler-path and completion-cost judgment above.
When a new protection lacks basis, obtain evidence, revise or withdraw it before
making it mandatory. When an existing protection lacks basis, do not delete it
or treat missing evidence as successful reuse. Limit only the adoption or
promotion conclusion that depends on that basis; unrelated work continues.
Established necessity, disproved necessity or scope, and insufficient evidence
must remain distinct conclusions under the existing revision owners and routes.
An established defect requires bounded correction and necessary consumer
adaptation through those owners. An evidence gap requires bounded investigation,
not expansion of the mechanism or an unsupported deletion.

## Stable routes

- `contract_incomplete`: return to Planning or repair for missing applicable
  contract, authority, decision, constitution, or check facts.
- `architecture_conflict`: return to Planning for an authority conflict.
- `fitness_regression`: return to implementation/check for a new or worsened
  deviation, owner expansion, dual writer, or closed-GAP recurrence.
- `sync_required`: return to promotion/repair for stale baseline,
  constitution, contribution, or expected-current identity.

The deterministic invocation records no authorization and makes no semantic
decision. It validates the AI-authored result and serializes exactly one closed
typed output.

`owner_not_yet_executed` is only an internal execution state: the current AI
owner continues the sequence above. Missing authority, constitution, project
contract, descriptor, or required check evidence uses the existing
`contract_incomplete`, `baseline_incomplete`, or `blocked` route as applicable.
A deterministic validation error is repaired by correcting the authored result
or freshly rereading stale identity/freshness facts only after the current
invocation reports the exact error and stops. That correction starts a new
semantic owner round with a newly authored envelope; it is not a retry within
the prior round and the failed invocation is never converted into an
Architecture judgment. Only a platform that cannot perform the required AI
review, public/live reads, result authoring, or formal invocation may report a
true execution-capability gap.

This standard-library recipe reads the caller's current public input file and
writes only the Planning freshness token to stdout. Run it from the repository
root; replace the example filename with that actual input. The printed newline
is not part of the digest.

```sh
python3 - planning-input.json <<'PY'
import hashlib
import json
import sys
from pathlib import Path

public = json.loads(Path(sys.argv[1]).read_text(encoding='utf-8'))
locator = public.get('design_locator') or public.get('candidate', {}).get('design_locator')
if locator:
    content = Path(locator).read_bytes()
else:
    content = json.dumps(public['candidate'], ensure_ascii=False, sort_keys=True,
                         separators=(',', ':')).encode('utf-8')
print('sha256:' + hashlib.sha256(content).hexdigest())
PY
```
