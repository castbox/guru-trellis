# Architecture Baseline: Planning 3.0 and Nonplanning 5.0

## Authority model

The business repository Architecture Baseline is the only shared architecture
SSOT. It owns current/target state, decisions, GAPs, owners, compatibility
exits, ADRs, history, evidence, and the design constitution or its unique
locator. Guru Team owns the mandatory lifecycle and typed routes, not the
project's architecture answer.

The constitution projection contains one explicit `current` or
`pending_reviewed_promotion` authority status, one authority locator, one
version or content identity, and exactly these five stable identity/name pairs:

- `mature-practice-applicability` / `成熟实践与适用性`
- `concept-semantic-completeness` / `概念与语义完整性`
- `cohesion-change-isolation` / `职责内聚与变化隔离`
- `minimum-necessary-complexity` / `最小必要复杂度`
- `debt-one-way-convergence` / `技术债务单向收敛`

Principle prose, scores, per-principle verdicts, and mechanical checklists are
not public Skill data.

### Adopted-mechanism review

Review mechanisms newly introduced, modified, reused or used more widely by the
candidate, together with their direct effects and consumers. A mechanism is a
choice carrying behavior or constraints, including data authority, responsibility,
coordination, failure handling or lifecycle. Reuse can bring those constraints
into a new path even when the original utility has no diff. Ordinary helpers
and causally unrelated dependencies do not each require a review unit. No actual
Architecture impact retains the original lightweight route.

First reconstruct supported normal completion and its necessary business, data,
permission and external constraints. After locating the design basis, independently
judge whether the adopted choice is necessary, appropriately scoped and worth its
total operating and maintenance cost. Establish the actual behavior it serves;
who decides, supplies facts and maintains state; and which objects and periods it
coordinates or rejects. Examine relevant shared writes, scans, blocking, retries,
keys, state, dependencies and recovery prerequisites. Compare feasible removal,
narrowing, replacement or retention and risk-matched evidence. These are focused
reasoning questions, not a required questionnaire, fixed sequence or global
optimization exercise. Finally check effects on legitimate normal requests.

Use applicable complete RDT and Architecture units under the shared
current-knowledge contract. Sufficient documentation can establish capability,
responsibility and applicability without repeated source archaeology. Read
implementation selectively when actual behavior, performance, the complete set
of affected consumers or conflicting documentation leaves a material gap. Finding
an explanation of how the mechanism works does not establish its necessity.
Reuse an applicable prior rationale and engineering facts concisely when they
cover the same constraints; stage or version changes alone do not require all
commands or all reasoning to be repeated.

Preserve real business and external contracts when comparing alternatives. When
an existing technical rule is itself under review, it cannot prove its own
necessity or exclude an alternative merely for omitting that rule. Evaluate the
candidate through the original reviewed-change contract, implement through its
lawful owner, and promote at the original stage. Do not change CURRENT first to
make the candidate conform or demand early promotion before implementation.
Existing, platform-native or installed capabilities and new code all require
current applicability. New abstractions, wrappers, conversions, configuration
and future provisions require a present responsibility and direct consumer.
Fewer keys or lines alone do not show lower total cost, especially when a new
ledger or manual coordination carries the displaced burden. A smaller diff must
not omit necessary responsibility boundaries, consumer adaptation or old-path exit.

For new or strengthened protection, consume the original
`guru-qualify-root-cause` contract's Protection judgment. Its proposer, including
an author, reviewer or template, bears the evidence burden. A missing technical
measure alone does not create a finding or validation obligation. Necessary
confidentiality or permission protection does not require a prior incident.
When a new protection lacks basis, obtain evidence, revise or withdraw it before
making it mandatory. When an existing protection lacks basis, do not delete it
or treat missing evidence as successful reuse. Limit only the adoption or
promotion conclusion that depends on that basis; unrelated work continues.
Established necessity, disproved necessity or scope, and insufficient evidence
must remain distinct conclusions under the existing revision owners and routes.

## Independent assessment entry

The first independent `task_impact_sync` assessment at `planning`, applicable
`implementation_discovery`, `phase2`, or `branch_review` uses a fresh independent
subagent, including a proposed no-impact change. The reviewer
has not authored or implemented the current candidate and has not read its PRD,
acceptance criteria, implementation summary, completion claims, prior pass, or
scope defense. Use empty task history, not a full-history fork of the caller.
Necessary AGENTS, engineering specs, this Skill, Architecture authorities and
operating instructions may load; they must not embed a task pass narrative.

The caller supplies exact repository/task locators, stage, current candidate
design locator or full-diff base/head, necessary long-term authority locators
and real constraint sources. These locate the object; they do not preselect
impact, change path, finding, pass or exit. Do not leak task narratives through
a dispatch summary. Planning reviews actual design facts and affected existing
implementation; `design.md` is a design object, not compliance authority. A
call-local design candidate is also valid. No real candidate means no candidate
pass; reading baseline authority alone is not design assessment.

Use the platform's supported fresh Task/Agent/subagent facility and inspect its
actual prelude. For Codex, use a fresh generic worker rather than an official
`trellis-check`, `trellis-implement` or `trellis-research` role whose task
context injection would preload narratives. A changed role name or different
worker id alone does not prove isolation. Do not patch upstream hooks or agent
definitions. If compliant dispatch is unavailable, the worker did not execute
or finish, or its result is missing/mismatched, stop through the existing
blocked/re-entry route; never degrade to the caller authoring the assessment.
Do not add a reviewer ledger, public reviewer fields or another state machine.

The reviewer reads and judges in this order:

1. current project constitution, Architecture Baseline and change contract;
2. actual Planning design facts, or the complete implementation candidate:
   Phase 2 includes tracked and untracked work; Branch Review binds the exact
   committed base/head/full diff;
3. changed decisions, defaults, state, rejection conditions, dependencies and
   responsibilities;
4. actual affected consumers, including unchanged callers, state reads/writes
   and runtime assembly;
5. comparable capabilities and their real constraints;
6. form an independent Architecture judgment, then inspect necessary design
   explanations/contribution for accurate attribution and responsibility.

After step 1 and before step 2, a receiving Delivery reviewer satisfies this
Skill's own session entry through original Identity/Ensure/Bind and explicit
Delivery Selection when those facts are missing. Recheck the same public key,
generation, phase, scope and root before continuing the original purpose.
This operational recovery uses the explicit public identity only; it does not
inject PRD, acceptance, implementation summary or previous pass into the
independent assessment. A different selection, terminal state or query error
stays with its original owner. Neither a key nor successful recovery supplies
an Architecture conclusion.

Prefer durable requirement/design/operations authority for business constraints.
When a necessary new constraint exists only in the task, obtain only that fact
and its source; do not import the whole task narrative. Missing constraints or
evidence remain conditional or use the existing incomplete/blocked route.

Keep normative constraints, source facts, accepted design and design assumptions
distinct. For an assumption that determines feasibility, responsibility,
rejection or a dependency, inspect its supporting original evidence and full
applicable conditions. A complete, applicable source excerpt with an identified
source and revision can supply that fact even when the excerpt is task-local.
Do not require another copy of the original document, an integration run or a
durable-source rewrite solely because the sufficient fact is task-local.
Judge any additional evidence need from the actual constraint or uncertainty.
If a necessary fact, source or applicability is missing, name that gap and
retain the existing incomplete/blocked route. A specification fact supports
only the judgment it covers; it does not prove actual external execution.

The reviewer independently determines the current complete scope, risks,
affected consumers and applicable project-check set before inspecting prior
execution results. It performs every applicable semantic project check for the
current invocation, authors the semantic result and invokes the formal wrapper.
Engineering commands supporting those checks follow the execution-fact rules
below; independent assessment does not require unconditional command repetition.
It returns the actual declared exit/output and current identity to the caller. Structure/schema/green commands,
prefilled owner results, worker labels, and caller self-filled Architecture

## Responsibility and change causality

Determine actual responsibility from the adopted decisions, consumer graph,
state ownership, assembly and dependency direction. Check whether a generic
capability interprets business semantics or treats protocol/model/endpoint/
default configuration as business authority. Compare similar capabilities with
their real constraints; existing implementation is evidence, not an approved
precedent. Evaluate a more direct alternative under the adopted-mechanism
review above, preserving real business and external contracts while allowing
the reviewed technical choice to change through its original contract.
Technical keywords and utility paths are not classifiers;
do not audit unrelated exported symbols or invent future consumers. A controlled
extension example may exercise an existing extension point.

Design/code/tests/contribution consistency, green acceptance, declared owner
agreement, approved plans or user continuation do not establish architectural
correctness. An evidenced current responsibility violation is itself a defect
without first requiring a functional failure. Distinguish an inherited error
from its new expansion or solidification with concrete before/after causality.
New/worsened deviations and necessary local boundary convergence belong to the
current change. Unrelated historical debt may be reported truthfully but does
not automatically become a current P0-P3 finding, release gate, required
follow-up or Issue. Insufficient evidence stays uncertain.

For an evidenced responsibility deviation, use the existing gate evidence to
state the concrete before/after causal delta and a more direct alternative.
Identify the actual owning boundary, necessary affected-caller adaptations and
obsolete-path exit, or explain from the current graph why any of these is not
needed. Preserve supported behavior and legal configuration, and stop at
causally unrelated debt. A generic "revise the mechanism" conclusion alone does
not explain the necessary local convergence. This is a causal correction
explanation for the delivery owner, not severity, a repair authorization, or a
new acceptance contract; no additional result fields or artifact are required.

When correcting an existing decision, identify its actual adopters, shared
state, data, interfaces, configuration and runtime dependencies, including
unchanged consumers. Apply the shared current-knowledge contract's Migration
and retirement method to the same decision's affected existing implementations. Coexistence that
breaks the same invariant, causes incompatible reads/writes or defeats the
correction requires necessary adaptation and exit through the original scope
and delivery owners. Independent retention needs evidenced coexistence and an
executable transition; a different scenario with a still-valid rationale may
remain within its stated scope. Analysis can exceed implementation scope and
does not authorize a repository-wide migration or cross-repository write.
New consumers must not expand a pattern already judged wrong. Promotion alone
does not prove old implementations migrated or the whole Task completed.

The reviewer reports Architecture facts and causal effects; delivery owners
separately determine current work and qualification before edits/tests/severity.
Neither scope defense nor discovery alone settles that responsibility or
authorizes changes. If a necessary correction changes product goals or
Architecture authority, stop the affected work through the existing owner route.

## Stage lifecycle

Planning assesses the real prewrite candidate before solution writing, then
Wording-current final facts immediately before Approval. Implementation discovery re-enters
when a material boundary expands. Phase 2 performs applicable project checks
and a first before/after semantic judgment. Branch Review independently
recomputes the same concerns from the committed full diff. Publication rejects
missing, stale, conflicting, incomplete, regressing, or unpromoted state.
Acceptance/Finish accepts only fresh no-change or reviewed+promoted state.

Promotion belongs to the original Architecture owner and binds expected-current.
If current advanced, the owner first assesses its real effect and uses the
existing sync route without overwriting. After final-integration writes, implementation rebuilds the public candidate
with the same fixed base and all actual paths, then calls begin_check. Final
Architecture/Check assess that new candidate; the prewrite candidate is stale. The committed Architecture assessment and full Branch
Review independently include every implementation and promotion file. The
resulting current identity is the only identity the next task may consume.

Only a `baseline_current` produced by `task_impact_sync` may resume the exact
matching stage. A current result from bootstrap or repair ordinarily reruns the
affected `task_impact_sync` stage; it does not resume that stage directly.
Final-integration maintenance instead returns to its original promotion as
defined below. A current
promotion result returns its actual promoted candidate for implementation to
refresh the public candidate and re-enter final Architecture/Check, then
Task Commit and independent committed full-diff Branch Review.

Bootstrap activates only a successor with the same baseline locator,
`status=active`, and an identity distinct from the missing, draft, or
superseded predecessor. Repair accepts only an already active baseline; it
cannot relabel a draft or superseded baseline as current.

## Final-integration promotion 5.0 / Delivery 3.0

This section replaces ordinary pre-Check promotion. `promotion` selects
schema_version=5.0; `delivery_promotion` selects delivery-3.0. Both require
stage=final_integration and sync_kind=promotion_required. Nonplanning impact
uses the matching 5.0 / delivery-3.0 input and owner-result schema. Planning's
two purpose-specific branches, bootstrap and repair retain their contracts.
The old 4.0 / delivery-2.0 schemas remain fixed historical validation assets.
The current runtime rejects those inputs. Use the matching complete installation
under MIGRATION-638 or retain the pinned complete old installation; do not
convert an old pass into a current result.

The public promotion input keeps Task/Delivery, mode/continuation, baseline,
constitution, project contract, freshness and contribution sync target. Its
baseline is the applicable active authority read for this operation.
expected_current_identity and current_identity equal that before identity.
Ordinary promotion can retain the same identity. It does not require a
successor_baseline, a write to the root entry or a root-entry content token.
Bootstrap alone retains its distinct-successor rule. Root navigation changes
only when identity, status or necessary relationships actually change. Git
commit plus locator identifies the exact applicable historical content.
There is no committed_range: the commit and independent Review occur later.

The public baseline and expected/current identity fields describe the authority
before this operation's writes. The existing owner-result and output
`baseline_identity` describe the actual active authority after them. The owner
reads the complete after authority and judges its identity and applicability.
For leaf-only writes the after identity equals the before identity. An actual
identity migration may return a different after identity only when the reviewed
target list includes the baseline locator with non-null, different before and
after content. The normal target readback, full candidate reconstruction and
postwrite applicability checks still apply. The validator checks this objective
write boundary; it does not parse Markdown to determine identity. Thus the first
numbered-to-stable migration can write its real baseline delta without requiring
future ordinary promotions to write that entry or create a successor field.

The first independent Architecture assessment and the original Check owner's
candidate-only check cover the complete implementation and proposed targets
before shared-current writes. `candidate_checked` enters final integration
only after the original Check owner legally returns the Delivery to
implementation. This is not a Git synchronization gate. Fix one actual target
branch/commit for this operation and read every affected complete unit and
necessary relationship/navigation. Preserve unaffected units and still-valid
conditions from intervening promotions. When the combined proposal changes,
the original independent reviewer supplements all actual deltas and affected
unchanged consumers under the shared candidate-applicability contract.
Changed requirement premises return to scope/Planning; changed implementation,
interfaces or verification duties return to their original quality owner.

Both current impact and promotion additionally supply:

- before_authority: git_revision, baseline_locator, constitution_locator and
  change_contract_locator, with optional absent_locators. The absence list is a
  unique subset of these three locator values. Omitted or empty means all three
  files exist at git_revision. The validator reads that fixed Git tree: listed
  paths must be absent and every unlisted path must be an existing readable
  authority file. These facts locate the immutable before state for comparison.
  When Planning/Bootstrap first establishes authority after that revision, the
  owner reviews the complete new current files and their full candidate delta.
  Declared absence never waives current authority, candidate or semantic gates. For committed Review, git_revision equals the fixed
  committed_range.base_head. New CURRENT is a reviewed change and cannot prove
  conformity to the original constraints.
- candidate: head and content_identity, where content_identity is `sha256:`
  followed by the existing guru-reviewed-content-1.0 digest. Capture it through
  the existing public reviewed-content identity command with --include-worktree;
  this is a local consistency token, not semantic evidence or authorization.
  Existing metadata exclusions remain unchanged. The reviewer still reads the
  complete tracked/untracked candidate and affected unchanged consumers.

The independent Phase 2 reviewer first reads the original authority, then
the actual complete candidate and all proposed Architecture target contents.
It determines responsibilities, dependencies, compatibility and validation
obligations before inspecting contribution attribution. Its private review
binds candidate and before_authority. The original owner rereads expected-current
and writes the reviewed contents to the current execution branch with ordinary
file tools. This package's invocation only records/validates; it never writes
Architecture targets or chooses a semantic route.

Current nonplanning structured contributions use architecture-contribution-5.0.
Its reviewed state can bind the independent pre-Check candidate and original
authority; it does not require the later committed range. Planning pending
contributions retain their original meaning. The 2.0 contribution schema remains
a pinned historical validation asset.

The call-local owner result includes promotion_targets, each with locator,
before_content and content (null denotes absence/deletion), plus post_candidate
and postwrite_applicability={status: applicable, reason}. The complete target
list is the exact composed delta reviewed by the independent reviewer. Before the
first write, the owner checks every current target equals its before_content.
After writing, the owner reads all actual targets and independently verifies
that no semantics, dependencies or validation obligations changed. Exact reviewed
writes require this original-owner applicability check, not another independent
same-content assessment. Project checks remain newly judged for the current
invocation; applicable engineering command facts may retain their original use.

The validator reads the real candidate, checks every actual target equals its
reviewed content, reconstructs the reviewed candidate using the original target
contents, and binds the actual post_candidate. An additional implementation
change, missing target or changed proposed content cannot project current.
The wrapper does not infer architectural correctness from byte equality.

After partial writes, the original owner reads the same intent and classifies
each actual target as completed, pending or conflict. When the original intent
is uniquely recoverable and all files are unconflicted, write only pending
contents and repeat postwrite applicability. On a conflict or missing/ambiguous
intent, stop writing and use the existing blocked route. Output loss after
complete writes may rematerialize the same result from still-current actual
files and original intent. No repeated completed write, conflict overwrite, second
state machine or persistent reviewer/authorization history is permitted.
The current invocation uses no checkpoint. If a separate recovery situation
needs a short-lived same-owner intent, retain only its non-reconstructible
target contents under existing ignored-runtime ownership and delete it when
its direct recovery consumer finishes. Never pass it downstream.

A current output exposes before_authority and the actual candidate only to the
unique current-router consumer. That original owner uses the candidate to bind
implementation's public candidate refresh and final Architecture/Check, or the matching current stage, and before_authority to retain the
original comparison authority for committed Review and subsequent eligibility.
Promotion projects post_candidate; impact projects its assessed candidate. Full
review contents, target bytes, checks and recovery intent remain private.

No-impact keeps no_change and adds no contribution, ADR, target intent or
write. Later findings return to the earliest affected owner according to actual
responsibility, behavior, compatibility and validation changes. Changed baseline
or contribution statements require their matching correction and current
Architecture result. An unchanged completed promotion is not repeated merely
because HEAD changed. A changed candidate never borrows the old reviewed_promoted
result; the original owner judges the needed assessment and checks afresh.

`stage=planning|phase2`. The phase2 input is the existing maintenance projection
