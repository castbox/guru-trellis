# Current Effective Knowledge

This is the shared reading and writing contract for the existing RDT and
Architecture maintenance owners. It changes document organization and owner
behavior, not public Skill inputs, profiles, schemas, exits or runtime state.
The project supplies its authority locators and actual migrated scope.

## Applicable authority and complete units

Before reading or changing knowledge, the AI owner selects the authority
applicable to the question, Task, stage and candidate. Read the repository's
authority entry and selected immutable version or Git revision. A later date,
larger version, search ranking or newer shared CURRENT does not establish
applicability. Preserve a Task's stage-applicable version unless a real
requirement, dependency, solution or explicitly applicable rule changes.
Promotion separately rereads its live shared target and expected-current.

Organize current knowledge into complete units by capability or responsibility.
Each unit has a stable identity, identifiable applicable version and status,
complete definition, necessary relationships, exact sources and replacement
boundaries. These are Markdown responsibilities, not a new record schema.
Existing canonical interface or engineering specifications keep their complete
definitions; a unit may reference them without copying their prose. The unit
must still state its own behavior and conditions. A link list or short summary
cannot stand in for that definition.

Retain every applicable condition, negation, exception, order, obligation,
compatibility limit and cross-document constraint. Distinguish confirmed
current behavior, accepted target behavior, unconfirmed material, history and
superseded definitions. An accepted target can constrain design without being
reported as implemented. Code-recovered or inferred statements keep their
provenance and uncertainty until the original owner reviews them.

Navigation contains identity, alias, status, locator/version and necessary
relationship only. It locates the unique body; it does not create another
business summary or decide which rule prevails. Preserve existing stable
identities independently of headings, file position or presentation order.
Ordinary reordering does not renumber unrelated knowledge.

## Reading for a question or stage

1. Resolve the applicable authority. Locate the capability through navigation,
   a stable reference or bounded search.
2. Read the complete relevant unit. Expand relationships necessary to answer
   or revise it: requirements/behavior, design responsibility/contract, test
   strategy/scenario/case and applicable Architecture constraints. The AI
   judges necessity; do not traverse every link without a reason.
3. Answer from applicable definitions with their conditions, status and exact
   sources. Read history for reasons, former behavior, or a documented
   unmigrated scope that still requires original complete sources. Ordinary
   migrated queries do not replay a contribution chain to assemble meaning.
4. If a source is missing, a reference is broken, definitions conflict or
   migration is incomplete, name the affected object and unsupported
   conclusion. Retain the last usable source and enter the original owner's
   repair, clarification or blocked route. Do not silently fill the gap from
   an apparent latest summary or report a complete answer.

Planning, implementation, final Check, independent Review and promotion use
this contract within their existing responsibilities. A prior semantic pass
is not the current stage's judgment.

## Revising and promoting

The RDT owner locates the changed behavior and necessary R/D/T relationships,
then judges Architecture impact through the original owner. The Architecture
owner locates affected decisions, responsibilities, consumers and project
selections. Preserve genuinely unaffected units after that judgment. Do not
manufacture related edits merely to fill all layers.

Classify the change as addition, modification, deletion, replacement, split or
merge. Keep surviving identities. Allocate new identities for new meanings.
Retain predecessor/successor locators for deleted, replaced, split or merged
objects. An old reference must resolve to its historical meaning and stated
transition; it must not silently acquire a new meaning. Update affected
bodies, necessary relationships and navigation together.

Ordinary parallel Tasks write their own isolated contributions. Each delta
identifies its applicable base, changed scope and complete proposed resulting
definition, directly or at a unique proposed-target locator. It does not edit
another Task's contribution or compete for shared current. Do not duplicate
unchanged authority into every delta.

At promotion, the original owner rereads the contribution and actual shared
target. Reconcile real intervening changes before writing. Integrate the
delta into the complete current unit, including still-effective predecessor
conditions and other promoted changes. Update R/D/T relationships and
navigation consistently. Retain contributions as provenance/history; do not
leave ordinary readers to assemble the promoted definition from them. A
changed expected-current uses the original sync/repair route and does not
authorize overwriting another contribution. Recovery remains with that owner
and its existing contract.

Architecture's independent Phase 2 assessment covers the complete candidate
and proposed targets. The original Architecture owner promotes and checks
actual-write applicability before final Check. Final Check, Task Commit and
independent committed full-diff Review cover the resulting content. This
contract adds no separate review owner or gate.

## Technical baseline and project projection

A reusable technical-stack baseline owns shared stack choices, constraints
and their scope in a real versioned source. A project projection identifies
that exact source/version and acquisition location, then owns project
selections, applicability and differences. Do not copy shared definitions or
let a project-only change rewrite the generic source or another project.
The original Architecture maintenance Skill works within each source's own
ownership boundary. A sanitized fixture illustrates the relationship; it
does not establish an external source or real adoption.

If the required source/version cannot be obtained, state the missing source
and affected selections. Keep unconfirmed choices distinct and use original
repair/clarification. A local inference or project projection cannot prove
the unavailable generic baseline's contents.

## Migration and retirement

Before cutover, identify the old authority/version, exact predecessor clauses,
target units, stable identities and relations, real old readers, switch
conditions and recovery. Compare the candidate with all still-effective
source constraints. Resolve overlapping or superseding statements
semantically; chronology alone cannot settle them. Switch the shared entry
only after original-owner completeness, traceability and applicable
verification gates are satisfied.

List actual migrated units and remaining unmigrated scope. For the latter,
retain exact original complete-source navigation and state the remaining
assembly boundary. Do not claim a whole corpus is migrated because several
representative units are complete. If cutover fails, keep the previous entry
and mark the candidate proposed/incomplete. After cutover, ordinary
corrections use existing repair or a reviewed successor; never rewrite an
immutable historical snapshot.

In-flight Tasks may continue reading the versions applicable to their plans
and candidates. Old-read support names those consumers, its scope and exit:
Task finish, legitimate reactivation/version reselection, or an actual
dependency change handled by the original owner. New changes maintain current
units; do not keep writing old current definitions indefinitely. Historical
Git/version evidence remains readable after ordinary old readers exit. It is
provenance, not a second active maintenance path.

Distribute this contract through existing complete package/preset paths.
Existing installations adopt matching workflow and packages, preserve local
content through managed conflict/sidecar handling, and verify reapply/drift.
Source parity proves distribution only. When accepted scope requires Agent
behavior or cost improvement, use equivalent actual old/new tasks, judge
complete answers first, and report repeated execution, loaded content,
maintenance work, failures and unverified boundaries separately.
