# Research: Official ASD-STE100 source and adoption limits

- Query: Verify a formal version and only those principles actually available from official sources.
- Scope: external
- Date: 2026-10-17

## Findings

Official STEMG site https://www.asd-ste100.org/ states: “ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English Issue 9 - January 15, 2025.” The operational site belongs to the ASD Simplified Technical English Maintenance Group. Its legal notice identifies ASD, Brussels, as copyright/trademark owner. The live About page confirms Issue 9, 53 writing rules in nine sections and a controlled dictionary. It says more than half the writing rules were refined in Issue 9. Do not infer Issue 9 numbered clauses from older editions.

### Adoption recommendation

Pin ASD-STE100 Issue 9 (2025-01-15) as English reference. Record the precise official FAQ question/section anchors above as the verified source for limited adapted principles. Use independent stable project rule identifiers (for example GTW-001), clearly marked mandatory/advisory, with applicable content and exceptions.

Mark numbered Issue 9 clauses and full dictionary as “not obtained/not adopted”; do not invent clause numbers or call Chinese compliant. Project expansions for conversations, requirements modality, evidence boundaries, exact code/path literals and Task/Delivery/Check/Review distinctions are project rules, not verified ASD-STE100 requirements.

The FAQ also confirms the next Issue 10 is scheduled for January 2028. This is a schedule, not a released standard or a reason to adopt future wording.

## Caveats / Not Found

The formal PDF is available only by a request form at https://forms.gle/7gqWUtj2UK2CBTBv5 according to the official downloads page; no public standard PDF link was exposed. No form was submitted and no message was sent. Numbered clauses were therefore not verified. The official public FAQ provides sufficient provenance for explicitly limited adapted principles, but does not support a claim that numbered standard clauses or full STE compliance were verified. The project must describe this boundary directly for AC1.
